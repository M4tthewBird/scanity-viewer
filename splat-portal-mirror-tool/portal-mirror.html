Add and manage portals - mirrors that reflect the splat scene and windows that show the HDRI.

  • Pick a shape to arm placement: the cursor turns into a crosshair, the first click in the scene sets the centre and the second sets the radius or corner.
  • If the first click lands on empty space, the portal spawns 1×1 in front of the camera, snapped to the nearest 90° - the old behaviour.
  • Esc or a right-click cancels an armed or in-progress placement.
  • New portals are mirrors; switch one to a window with Type in Portal Settings.
  • Click a portal in the list to select and frame it; the eye toggle hides or shows it.
  • Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy and duplicate the selected portal; Delete removes it.
  • Export mirrors.json downloads every mirror portal's transform and shape as a JSON file (window portals are skipped) - point a viewer's own renderer at this file to reproduce the mirrors without this tool.

Transform

Position, rotate, and scale the selected portal.

  • Pick Move / Rotate / Scale (or press T / R) to transform with the viewport gizmo.
  • Toggle World / Local space and Snap from the toolbar on the right.
  • Type exact X / Y / Z values into the fields; the padlock locks proportional scale.
  • Each gold reset dot restores that row to the portal's spawn value.

Define the portal's type, shape, and how it cuts through the splats.

  • Type switches between Mirror (live reflection) and Window (shows a flat colour or the HDRI).
  • Shape sets the outline - circle, rectangle, or capsule - and its size.
  • Cull Front / Back carve a slab of splats away around the portal so its surface is visible.
Mirror Settings
1

Only appears while a mirror is selected, but every setting here is scene-wide - they apply to all portals, not just this one.

  • Portals Active is the master toggle: off gives you the raw splat scene, with no reflections, holes or clipping.
  • Render All reflects every on-screen mirror instead of only the one you look at.
  • Mirror Resolution lowers reflection resolution to trade sharpness for speed.

What windows show. Only appears while a window portal is selected, but every setting here is scene-wide.

  • Color is what windows show with the HDRI off - a flat, unlit fill shared by all of them.
  • Enable switches every window over to the 360° environment instead of its flat colour.
  • HDR Map picks a built-in equirect map; Load Custom takes your own .hdr / .jpg.
  • Brightness and Rotation adjust that environment for every window at once.
